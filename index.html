AI music films, made with a pipeline I built

Since July 2026 · 14 films

Historical portraits, life stories, a love story, travel diaries, Zhuangzi's essay and one deep-sea fantasy. A typical film runs about three to four minutes, cut from 36 storyboarded shots, delivered in 4K with Chinese–English subtitles and a credit for every AI model used. They all go through the same pipeline: 7 steps and 22 tasks, packaged as 30 Claude Code skills. Every mistake goes into a log that now holds 886 lessons, and I sign off every checkpoint myself.

Vdanmu 微弹幕, co-founded in college

2014–2017 · Wuhan · Co-founder & product lead

Vdanmu put an audience's live WeChat messages onto the speaker's big screen as scrolling danmaku (bullet comments), at campus galas, company annual meetings and in classrooms. I co-founded it in my second year of college and led product. We reached 300,000+ users in under three years and raised a US$150K seed round, and I co-invented two Chinese invention patents on it (filed by HUST, granted 2018).
